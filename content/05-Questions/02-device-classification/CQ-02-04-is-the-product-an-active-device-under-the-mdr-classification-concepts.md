# Is the product an active device under the MDR classification concepts?

## Ontology path

`DeviceConfiguration → EnergySource/Action → ActiveDeviceCharacteristic → ClassificationRule`


## Answer

The product is an active device when its operation depends on a source of energy other than energy generated by the human body for that purpose or gravity and it acts by changing or converting that energy. The ontology evaluates the configuration’s energy source and action, records the active-device characteristic, and applies the relevant classification rules.
