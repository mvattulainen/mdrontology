# Use-case demonstrations

These pages turn the use-case catalogue into five concrete journeys through the synthetic infusion-pump data. Together they cover pre-market compliance, release control, product change, post-market feedback and explainable decision support.

> [!important]
> Demonstrations explain what the ontology can support. They are engineering examples, not legal conclusions or substitutes for qualified regulatory review.

## Explore

1. [Regulatory compliance gap assessment](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-demo-02-regulatory-compliance-gap-assessment) — find the missing evidence behind an open GSPR instance.
2. [Market-release readiness](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-demo-03-market-release-readiness) — decide whether the current configuration can pass its release gate.
3. [Change-impact analysis](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-demo-05-change-impact-analysis) — expose the regulatory consequences of a software change.
4. [PMS and vigilance closed loop](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-demo-08-pms-vigilance-closed-loop) — follow a post-market signal into change control and recurring reporting.
5. [Grounded LLM regulatory assistant](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-demo-18-grounded-llm-regulatory-assistant) — assemble an answer packet without inventing missing facts.

## Why these five

[The impact assessment](/07_other/use-case-demonstrations/uc-assess-001-use-case-impact-assessment) scores all 19 proposed use cases and explains the selection. Classification and explainability remain visible inside the five demonstrations even where they are not separate pages.
