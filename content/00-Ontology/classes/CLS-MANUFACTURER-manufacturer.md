# Manufacturer

## Definition

**Terminology type:** MDR

A natural or legal person that manufactures or fully refurbishes a device, or has a device designed, manufactured or fully refurbished, and markets it under its name or trademark. In the ontology this is the organisation that ultimately bears the MDR manufacturer obligations for the device.
