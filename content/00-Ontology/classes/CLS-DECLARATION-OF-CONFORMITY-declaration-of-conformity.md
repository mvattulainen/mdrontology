# Declaration Of Conformity

## Definition

**Terminology type:** MDR

The manufacturer's formal EU declaration stating that the applicable MDR requirements have been fulfilled for the device(s) covered. It is issued by the manufacturer after completion of the applicable conformity-assessment obligations.
