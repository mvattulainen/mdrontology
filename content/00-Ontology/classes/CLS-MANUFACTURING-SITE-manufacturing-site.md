# Manufacturing Site

## Definition

**Terminology type:** Ontology

A controlled physical facility at which manufacturing, assembly, testing, packaging, sterilisation, release or another manufacturing activity is carried out. A manufacturer may have several manufacturing sites, and outsourced sites may belong to suppliers.
