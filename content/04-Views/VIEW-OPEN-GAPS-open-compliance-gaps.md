## Resolve a gap

Open the subject requirement, inspect the failed constraint, add configuration-scoped evidence or an explicit justified state, then rebuild the ontology.

Hard findings can block a lifecycle decision. Advisory findings identify data-quality improvements without asserting non-compliance, while review-trigger rules request qualified assessment rather than producing a final legal conclusion.
