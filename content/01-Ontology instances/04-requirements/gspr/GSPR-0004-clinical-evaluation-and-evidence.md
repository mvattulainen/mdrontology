# Clinical evaluation and evidence

## Purpose

Represents the reusable obligation to plan, conduct, document and maintain clinical evaluation using evidence appropriate to the device and its intended purpose.

## Requirement use

Device-specific instances connect the obligation to configuration-scoped clinical-evaluation evidence and conclusions.
