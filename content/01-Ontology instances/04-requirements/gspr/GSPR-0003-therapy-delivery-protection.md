# Therapy-delivery protection

## Purpose

Represents the reusable Annex I obligation to control therapy-delivery hazards, including occlusion and air-in-line conditions, through appropriate detection, protective functions and verification.

## Requirement use

Device-specific compliance-requirement instances define applicability, acceptance criteria and objective evidence for each configuration.
